# First steps

Your first novel in Dante, in ten minutes. Shortcuts are written first as on a Mac, then as on Windows and Linux, for example Cmd+Enter / Ctrl+Enter. The full guide is in Italian for now: [Guida](guida.md).

1. **Create the project.** On the start screen press **New project**, write the title, press **Choose a folder…** and then **Create**. Dante makes a folder named after the novel, with a first chapter and a first scene. To just look around, press **Try the sample project** instead.
2. **Write.** Click in the scene and write. Dante saves as you type: there is no Save button.
3. **Split into scenes and chapters.** With the cursor where you want to cut, Cmd+Enter / Ctrl+Enter moves the text after it into a new scene; Cmd+Shift+Enter / Ctrl+Shift+Enter starts a new chapter. You can also use **Scene** and **Chapter** at the top of the tree on the left.
4. **Name your scenes.** Double-click a name in the tree (or press F2). In the panel on the right write the **synopsis**, two lines on what happens, and choose the **status**: idea, draft, revised or done. The dot beside the scene in the tree takes the colour of the status.
5. **Create a character.** Open the **Story Bible** (the book in the bar on the left) and press **New Character**. In a scene type `[[` and the start of the name: Dante suggests it and the name becomes a link to the card.
6. **See the novel from above.** In **Tables and boards** the **Board** shows the scenes by status: drag a scene to another column to change it. The **Scenes** table lists them with chapter, status, point of view and words.
7. **Export.** In **Export** choose Word, PDF, EPUB or Markdown and press **Export**. The file goes in the project's `export` folder.

**Good to know.** The novel is a folder of text files: you can keep it on Drive or Dropbox, open it with Obsidian and work on it with others. What you delete goes to the **Trash**, at the bottom of the bar on the left. When you close the project Dante saves a backup copy of it in Documents/Dante backup. The guide opens at any time from the question mark in the bar on the left.
